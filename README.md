Credit Risk Analysis

- Project Overview:

This project aims to analyze credit risk and identify patterns associated with customer default.

Using historical credit data, we'll explore customer profiles, payment behavior, credit limits, and other financial variables to better understand the factors related to credit risk.

- Business Problem:

A financial institution wants to better understand the characteristics and behaviors associated with customer default.

The objective is to use data analysis to identify risk patterns and support better credit decision-making.
